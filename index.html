Survival Analysis & Visualization Simplified

Identify robust cut-points, perform survival analysis, analyze clinical associations and generate publication-ready plots withouth writing a sinlge line of R code.

Download SCRIBE Watch Demo

Software features

Rank Markers

Upload multiple datasets simultaneously. Automatically rank markers based on ranges significantly associated with survival outcomes.

Optimal Cut-points

Visualize percentiles vs. p-values or hazard ratios to find the most statistically significant cut-points across your data.

Survival Plots

Instantly generate publication-ready Kaplan-Meier curves. Simply click your chosen cut-point to render the graph.

Cox Regression

Perform univariate or multivariate marker analysis and generate Forest Plots with the results

Correlation Analysis

Generate clean correlation matrices to understand relationships between different biological markers or variables.

Bar Graphs

Compare markers or patient categories using fully customizable bar plots exported in high resolution.

Contingency Tables

Automatically calculate patient counts split by marker medians and categories for rapid cohort analysis.
